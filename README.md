# Swedish Flashcards

A simple study app for the 50 most common Swedish words. Everything is in one file: `index.html`.

- **Swipe Cards**: tap to flip. Swipe right if you knew it, left if you didn't.
- **Multiple Choice**: pick the right meaning out of 4.
- **Typing**: type the word. Use the å ä ö buttons.
- The **Svenska → English** button at the top switches the direction.

Your weak words are saved on your device.

To add words, open `index.html` and follow the comment above the `WORDS` list.
